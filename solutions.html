Solutions to Prevent Water Pollution

1. Reduce Plastic Usage

Avoid single-use plastics and dispose of waste properly.

2. Treat Wastewater

Industries and communities should treat wastewater before disposal.

3. Protect Rivers and Lakes

Keep water resources clean and prevent dumping of waste.

4. Use Eco-Friendly Products

Choose environmentally friendly cleaning and farming products.

5. Save Water

Use water responsibly and prevent unnecessary wastage.

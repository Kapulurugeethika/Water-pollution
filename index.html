About Water Pollution

Water pollution occurs when harmful substances such as waste, chemicals, sewage and plastics enter rivers, lakes, ponds and groundwater. It affects humans, animals, plants and the entire environment. Clean water is essential for every living organism.

Major Causes of Water Pollution

Plastic Waste

Plastic bags and bottles thrown into water bodies cause serious environmental damage.

Industrial Waste

Untreated industrial chemicals can contaminate surface water and groundwater.

Domestic Sewage

Improper disposal of household wastewater can introduce harmful germs into water.

Agricultural Runoff

Fertilisers and pesticides may flow from agricultural land into nearby water sources.

Effects of Water Pollution

Human Health

Contaminated drinking water can spread diseases such as diarrhoea, cholera and typhoid.

Marine Life

Pollution can harm fish and other aquatic organisms and disturb food chains.

Environment

Polluted water damages ecosystems and reduces the availability of clean water.

Economy

Water pollution can affect fishing, agriculture, tourism and community livelihoods.

Solutions to Water Pollution

🚰 Treat Wastewater

Treat sewage and industrial wastewater before releasing it into the environment.

♻️ Reduce Plastic

Avoid single-use plastics and dispose of waste in appropriate bins.

🌱 Plant Trees

Protect green spaces and plant trees to support healthy ecosystems.

📢 Create Awareness

Educate communities about clean water, sanitation and responsible waste disposal.

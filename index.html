About Water Pollution

Water pollution occurs when harmful substances such as waste, chemicals, plastics and sewage enter rivers, lakes and other water sources. It affects human health, aquatic life and the environment. Clean water is essential for every living being.

Major Causes of Water Pollution

🏭 Industrial Waste

Factories may release harmful chemicals and untreated wastewater into nearby water sources.

🗑️ Plastic Waste

Plastic bags, bottles and other waste pollute rivers, lakes and ponds.

🚰 Sewage

Untreated sewage and household wastewater can contaminate water.

🌾 Agricultural Waste

Excess fertilizers and pesticides can enter water bodies through rainwater runoff.

Effects of Water Pollution

Human Health

Contaminated water can spread diseases and create serious health problems.

Aquatic Life

Pollution damages aquatic habitats and threatens fish and other organisms.

Environment

Pollution reduces water quality and damages natural ecosystems.

Prevention Methods

  • Do not throw plastic and garbage into water bodies.
  • Treat sewage and industrial wastewater before discharge.
  • Reduce the use of harmful chemical fertilizers and pesticides.
  • Keep rivers, lakes and ponds clean.
  • Promote rainwater harvesting.
  • Conduct community awareness programmes.
  • Encourage everyone to save water.

Community Awareness Survey

A community survey can help understand people's awareness of water pollution and clean water practices.

Survey Questions

  1. Do you know the causes of water pollution?
  2. Do you use safe drinking water?
  3. How do you dispose of household waste?
  4. Do you participate in cleanliness activities?
  5. What steps can improve water quality in your area?

About Water Pollution

Water pollution occurs when harmful substances such as chemicals, plastic, sewage and waste enter rivers, lakes, oceans and groundwater.

It affects aquatic life, human health and the environment. Protecting water resources is everyone's responsibility.

Major Causes of Water Pollution

Plastic Waste

Plastic waste contaminates water bodies and threatens aquatic organisms.

Industrial Waste

Factories may release harmful chemicals into nearby water sources.

Agricultural Waste

Fertilizers and pesticides can reach rivers and groundwater.

Effects of Water Pollution

Water pollution causes several environmental and health problems.

  • Damage to aquatic ecosystems
  • Spread of waterborne diseases
  • Loss of aquatic biodiversity
  • Contamination of drinking water
  • Environmental imbalance

Solutions to Water Pollution

Recycle Waste

Reduce plastic usage and recycle household materials.

Protect Nature

Keep rivers, lakes and surroundings clean and green.

Create Awareness

Educate communities about the importance of clean water.

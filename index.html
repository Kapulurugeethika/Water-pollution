Promoting Clean Water and Environmental Awareness in Society

A Community Service Project focused on water conservation, pollution prevention and community awareness.

About Our Community

Our project focuses on understanding water pollution problems in the community, collecting local information and creating awareness about protecting water resources. Community participation is important for maintaining clean and safe water.

Six Weeks of Project Activities

Week 1 – Planning

Project planning, area selection and questionnaire preparation.

Week 2 – Community Survey

Household visits, survey questions and data collection.

Week 3 – Awareness

Creating awareness about water pollution and clean water.

Week 4 – Detailed Survey

Observing local water-related concerns and recording responses.

Week 5 – Community Activities

Documenting awareness activities and community participation.

Week 6 – Assessment

Reviewing observations, feedback and project outcomes.

Findings and Solutions

Water Pollution

Waste disposal and untreated wastewater can affect local water quality.

Public Awareness

Community education can encourage responsible water use.

Clean Water

Proper waste management and water protection help maintain a healthy environment.

Suggested Actions

• Avoid throwing waste into water bodies.

• Keep surroundings and water sources clean.

• Promote safe water practices.

• Encourage community participation.

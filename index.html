Welcome to Our Project

Water is one of the most important natural resources for all living organisms. Water pollution occurs when harmful substances enter rivers, lakes and other water resources.

This website explains the causes, effects and prevention methods of water pollution. Our aim is to create awareness and encourage people to protect clean water resources.

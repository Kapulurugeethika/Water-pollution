About Water Pollution

Water pollution is the contamination of rivers, lakes, oceans and groundwater by harmful substances. These substances include chemicals, plastics, sewage and industrial waste. Water pollution reduces water quality and makes it unsafe for drinking, agriculture and aquatic life. Protecting water resources is essential for a healthy environment and sustainable future.

Causes of Water Pollution

Industrial Waste

Factories release harmful chemicals and untreated wastewater into water bodies.

Plastic Waste

Plastic materials pollute rivers and oceans and threaten aquatic animals.

Sewage Water

Untreated sewage introduces harmful microorganisms into water resources.

Agricultural Chemicals

Fertilizers and pesticides can enter rivers and lakes through agricultural runoff.

Oil Spills

Oil pollution damages marine ecosystems and affects aquatic organisms.

Types of Water Pollution

Surface Water Pollution

Contamination of rivers, lakes, ponds and streams.

Groundwater Pollution

Pollution that enters underground water through soil.

Marine Pollution

Contamination of oceans and seas by plastics, chemicals and oil.

Chemical Pollution

Harmful chemical substances entering natural water resources.

Effects of Water Pollution

Human Health Problems

Contaminated water can spread diseases and cause serious health problems.

Death of Aquatic Animals

Polluted water affects fish and other aquatic organisms.

Environmental Damage

Pollution disturbs aquatic ecosystems and reduces biodiversity.

Shortage of Clean Water

Pollution reduces the availability of safe drinking water.

Prevention of Water Pollution

🚯 Avoid Plastic

Never throw plastic waste into rivers and lakes.

🏭 Treat Wastewater

Industries should treat wastewater before releasing it.

🌱 Reduce Chemicals

Use fertilizers and pesticides responsibly.

🌊 Keep Water Clean

Participate in cleaning rivers, lakes and ponds.

📢 Spread Awareness

Educate people about protecting water resources.

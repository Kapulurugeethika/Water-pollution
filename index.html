Project overview

Water is one of the most important natural resources for all living organisms. Water pollution occurs when harmful substances such as chemicals, plastic waste and sewage enter rivers, lakes and other water sources.

This project focuses on understanding water pollution problems, creating awareness among people and encouraging responsible practices to protect water resources in society.

What we set out to do

Raise awareness

Explain the causes and effects of water pollution and the importance of clean water.

Listen to residents

Use community surveys and household visits to understand people's knowledge and concerns about water pollution.

Promote clean water

Encourage proper waste disposal and protection of rivers, lakes, ponds and other water sources.

Encourage participation

Motivate community members to take part in environmental awareness and cleanliness activities.

Causes and Effects of Water Pollution

Industrial Waste

Industrial wastewater can introduce harmful substances into water sources.

Plastic Waste

Plastic materials can contaminate rivers, lakes and ponds.

Domestic Sewage

Untreated sewage can reduce water quality and affect public health.

Agricultural Chemicals

Fertilizers and pesticides can enter water bodies through runoff.

Prevention and Solutions

Proper Waste Disposal

Do not throw plastic and household waste into water bodies.

Wastewater Treatment

Encourage appropriate treatment of wastewater before discharge.

Water Conservation

Use water responsibly and avoid unnecessary wastage.

Public Awareness

Encourage people to protect local water resources.

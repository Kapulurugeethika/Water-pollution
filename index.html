About Our Project

Water is one of the most important natural resources. Water pollution occurs when harmful substances enter rivers, lakes, ponds and other water sources. Our project aims to create awareness about clean water, environmental protection and community participation.

Our Main Objectives

  • Understand the causes of water pollution.
  • Study its effects on people and nature.
  • Understand community awareness about clean water.
  • Encourage responsible waste disposal.
  • Promote water conservation practices.
  • Encourage community participation.

Causes of Water Pollution

Several human activities contribute to the pollution of water resources.

🏭 Industrial Waste

Untreated industrial wastewater can introduce harmful substances into water sources.

🗑️ Plastic Waste

Plastic bags, bottles and garbage can contaminate rivers, lakes and ponds.

🚰 Domestic Sewage

Improper disposal of household wastewater can reduce water quality.

🌾 Agricultural Chemicals

Fertilizers and pesticides can enter water bodies through surface runoff.

Effects of Water Pollution

Human Health

Unsafe water can spread waterborne diseases and create health problems.

Aquatic Life

Pollution can damage aquatic habitats and affect fish and other organisms.

Environment

Pollution affects ecosystems and reduces the quality of natural water resources.

Prevention and Solutions

Everyone has a role in protecting water resources. Small responsible actions can make a difference.

Keep Water Sources Clean

Do not throw plastic, garbage or other waste into rivers and ponds.

Treat Wastewater

Ensure wastewater is treated properly before it is released into water bodies.

Save Water

Avoid unnecessary water wastage and encourage conservation.

Create Awareness

Share information about water pollution and environmental protection.

Community Survey

Use these questions to understand community awareness about water pollution.

  1. Are you aware of the causes of water pollution?
  2. What is your main source of drinking water?
  3. Do you know the importance of safe drinking water?
  4. How do you dispose of household waste?
  5. What steps can improve water cleanliness in your area?
  6. Would you participate in water conservation activities?

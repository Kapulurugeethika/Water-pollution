Effects of Water Pollution

1. Human Health Problems

Contaminated water can spread diseases and cause health problems.

2. Death of Aquatic Animals

Polluted water harms fish and other aquatic organisms.

3. Environmental Damage

Water pollution damages natural ecosystems and biodiversity.

4. Shortage of Clean Water

Pollution reduces the availability of safe water for communities.

5. Economic Problems

Pollution affects fishing, farming and tourism activities.

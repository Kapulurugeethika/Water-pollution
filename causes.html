Causes of Water Pollution

1. Industrial Waste

Factories release harmful chemicals and untreated waste into rivers and lakes.

2. Plastic Waste

Plastic bottles, bags and other materials pollute water bodies.

3. Agricultural Chemicals

Fertilizers and pesticides can enter nearby water resources.

4. Sewage Disposal

Untreated sewage can contaminate drinking water sources.

5. Oil Spills

Oil contamination affects aquatic ecosystems.
